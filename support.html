Support

Email contact.umutfidan@gmail.com — we usually reply within two business days.

If you are writing about a purchase, open Settings in the app, tap the Support ID card to copy it, and paste it into your email. Condbend has no accounts, so that ID is the only way we can find your purchase.

I paid but Pro is still locked

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases. This re-checks your App Store or Google Play account. If it does not help, make sure you are signed in with the same store account you purchased with, then email us your Support ID.

I changed phones

Install Condbend, open Settings, and tap Restore purchases. Subscriptions and the lifetime unlock follow your store account, not the device.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store, not by us:

  • iPhone: Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Condbend.
  • Android: Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Condbend.

Cancelling during the free trial means you are not charged. Pro stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.

A result does not match my bender or my chart

This is the feedback we most want. Bending results are shown as the traditional field-table numbers first, with the exact trigonometric value alongside, and take-up is bender-specific and editable in the app. If a number still looks wrong, email us with the calculator, the inputs you used, the value you expected, and where that expected value came from.

Condbend is a reference tool, not a substitute for the electrical code or professional judgment. Wire sizing, conduit fill, and box fill results are estimates for planning only. Always verify against the current NEC / CEC / BS 7671 and your local amendments, and confirm take-up against your own bender before cutting or bending.

Requesting a refund

Refunds are handled by the stores. On iOS use reportaproblem.apple.com; on Android use the Play Store order history. Email us as well and we will help where we can.
