Delete your Condbend account

Condbend, by Umut Fidan

A Condbend account is created when you sign in with Apple or Google to buy or restore Condbend Pro. The free calculators never create one. You can delete it yourself in the app, or ask us to delete it without the app.

In the app

  1. Open Condbend and tap Settings (top-right icon).
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account and confirm.
  3. Sign in with Apple or Google once more when asked — the provider requires a fresh sign-in before an account can be deleted.

Without the app

Email contact.umutfidan@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Condbend account, from the email address you signed in with. If you used Sign in with Apple with “Hide My Email”, include your Support ID (Settings → Account) or the relay address instead. We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account: user ID, name and email address — in the app or by email.
  • For Sign in with Apple, Condbend's access to your Apple ID is revoked.
  • On an email request, also the purchase record kept under that user ID by our purchase processor, RevenueCat.

What is kept

  • Crash reports are not linked to your account; they are deleted automatically by Firebase Crashlytics after 90 days.
  • Apple and Google keep their own records of your purchases.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings first, otherwise it keeps renewing.

A purchase belongs to your App Store or Google Play account, not to your Condbend account. Sign in again — with any account — on a device using that same store account and tap Restore purchases to get Pro back. Pro granted by support cannot be recovered.
