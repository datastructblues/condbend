Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

The short version. Condbend does its work entirely on your phone. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, and no advertising or behavioural tracking. Your measurements and settings never leave your device. Two things do leave it: what is needed to process and restore a purchase, and an automatic report if the app crashes.

Who we are

Condbend is developed by Umut Fidan (DataStructBlues). You can reach us at contact.umutfidan@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following in its own private storage so it behaves sensibly between sessions. This data is never transmitted to us or to anyone else, and it is removed when you uninstall the app:

  • Your unit system (imperial or metric) and theme preference.
  • Whether you have completed the introduction screen.
  • The values you last entered in each calculator, so a screen reopens where you left it.
  • A flag recording whether Pro is unlocked.

Information related to purchases

If you buy a subscription or the lifetime unlock, purchase processing is handled by Apple or Google, and subscription state is managed for us by RevenueCat, Inc. We never see or receive your card number, billing address, or Apple/Google account details.

To make purchases work across sessions and support restoring them, RevenueCat receives:

  • A randomly generated, anonymous identifier for your installation. It is not derived from your name, email, phone number, or any advertising ID, and we cannot use it to identify you personally.
  • The purchase receipt issued by the App Store or Google Play, and the status of your subscription.
  • Basic technical context such as device platform, app version, and store country.

RevenueCat processes this on our behalf as a service provider. See the RevenueCat privacy policy, the Apple privacy policy, and the Google privacy policy for how each of them handles data.

Crash reports

When the app crashes, a diagnostic report is sent automatically so the fault can be found and fixed. This is handled by Firebase Crashlytics, a Google service. A report contains:

  • The stack trace of the crash — which lines of our code failed.
  • Device model, operating system version, and app version.
  • The state of the device at the time, such as available memory and whether the app was in the foreground.
  • A Firebase installation identifier: a random, resettable ID for the app on your device. It is not your name, email, or an advertising ID, and reinstalling the app produces a new one.

Crash reports are used only to fix defects. We do not enable Google Analytics, so no behavioural or usage events are collected alongside them. See the Firebase privacy documentation for how Google processes this data.

Because the calculators run offline and hold no personal data, a crash report cannot contain your measurements or job details.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or location.
  • We do not use advertising, behavioural analytics, or cross-app tracking SDKs, and there are no ads in the app.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

Children

Condbend is a professional tool for electricians and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your choices and rights

Because we hold no information that identifies you, there is normally nothing personal for us to look up, correct, or delete. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your device, and the installation identifier attached to any crash report is discarded with it.

If you want the anonymous purchase record associated with your installation deleted, email us from any address and include the Support ID shown in the app under Settings. Note that deleting it also removes the record of your purchase, so Pro would no longer restore on that device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
